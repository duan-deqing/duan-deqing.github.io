- **Leaner routing and App**: the route table, lazy pages and idle prefetch of the article page moved to `src/routes.jsx`; `App.jsx` now only wires the settings provider, scroll reset and routes.
- **Shared shell components**: extracted `WorkspaceShell`, `SideRail` and `PageContainer`, so the blog, bookmarks and subpage layouts share one sidebar, scroll area and content width; layout files are much shorter.
- **Split stylesheets**: `index.css` now only imports; fonts and palette, the base layer and shared classes live in `styles/tokens.css`, `styles/base.css` and `styles/components.css`.
- **Dead code cleanup**: removed the unused `SocialIcon` / `ArrowIcon`, `DownloadIcon`, uncalled article helpers and redundant component props.
